Missing Marks

See which learners are missing marks in your subjects and fill them in here.

📌 How it works
Choose one of your classes. An assessment (AOI 1–4 or EOT; Paper 1 / Paper 2 for A-Level) counts as posted for a subject once at least one learner has a mark for it. Every learner without that mark is listed with exactly what is missing. Type the missing marks and tap Submit Missing Marks; they are saved the same way as Submit Results (AOI out of 20, EOT out of 80, papers out of 100).
